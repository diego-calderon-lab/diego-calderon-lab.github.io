# Trevor Fox
## Lab Manager / Junior Specialist

I am the Lab Manager and a Junior Specialist in the Calderon Lab, where I oversee day-to-day lab operations and conduct research. I graduated with a B.A. in Molecular and Cell Biology from the University of California, Berkeley, where I worked in the Harland Lab as both an undergraduate researcher and later a Staff Research Associate. My previous research focused on determining whether molecular drivers of gastrulation are evolutionarily conserved across vertebrate models, including *Xenopus laevis*, zebrafish, chicken, and axolotl. My current research interests focus on gene regulatory elements, epigenetics, and their roles in development, cell identity, and disease.

Outside the lab, I enjoy visiting used record stores, baking, hiking, and playing board games with friends.
